# Week 1 fact check (researched 2026-11-09)

## Day 1 - Space: HD 189733 b rains glass sideways
- Verified claim: a deep-blue hot Jupiter about 63-64 light-years away. Its blue color comes from silicate (glass) particles scattering blue light. The air is hot enough to melt silicates into glass droplets, and very fast winds carry them sideways. In 2024, JWST detected hydrogen sulfide in its atmosphere (Nature, 8 July 2024).
- Sources:
  - NASA: https://science.nasa.gov/?p=203588
  - Univ. of Maryland: https://www.astro.umd.edu/news-events/news/nearby-exoplanet-reeks-rotten-eggs-and-thats-good-thing
- Nuance:
  - Wind speed: NASA gives about 4,500 mph and UMD about 5,000 mph. The script says "about five thousand". The popular 5,400 mph figure was not used.
  - Temperature: NASA gives about 2,000 F (~1,090 C) and UMD about 930 C. The script says "around a thousand degrees Celsius".
  - The glass rain is inferred from models and spectra. Nobody has seen it directly.
  - "Smells like rotten eggs" is a rhetorical image based on the H2S detection.

## Day 2 - Human body: you glow
- Verified claim: living organisms give off ultraweak photon emission (UPE), a by-product of metabolism through reactive oxygen species.
  - A 2009 PLoS ONE study (Kobayashi et al.) imaged it from human bodies with a cryogenic CCD camera. The light was about 1,000 times below what the naked eye can detect.
  - A 2025 University of Calgary study (J. Phys. Chem. Lett.) found living mice emitted much more UPE than dead mice. Damaged areas of leaves emitted more light than undamaged areas.
- Sources:
  - https://www.sciencefocus.com/news/all-living-things-faintly-glow-ultraweak-photon-emission-upe
  - https://doaj.org/article/c77ea97939b64f4986150a3145cd2aea (Kobayashi 2009 abstract)
- Nuance:
  - The 2025 study measured mice and plants, not humans. The human glow claim comes from the 2009 study.
  - Calgary describes the light as 1,000 to 1,000,000 times dimmer than eyes can see.
  - The glow does not prove "auras" (the researchers say so explicitly).
  - It "dropped sharply" after death rather than vanishing completely, so the script says "faded".

## Day 3 - Animals: wombats poop cubes
- Verified claim: wombat droppings are cube-shaped. A 2021 Soft Matter study (Univ. of Tasmania and Georgia Tech) found the shape forms in the last 17% of the colon. Two stiffer and two more flexible regions around the gut, together with drying and muscle contractions, produce the corners. Wombats leave the cubes on prominent spots such as rocks and logs to communicate.
- Sources:
  - https://www.utas.edu.au/news/2021/1/28/1109-scientists-get-to-the-bottom-of-wombat-cubed-poo-mystery/
  - https://www.australiangeographic.com.au/news/2021/02/why-do-wombats-have-cube-shaped-poo-scientists-may-have-solved-the-mystery/
- Nuance:
  - The corners are rounded-ish. The cubes are not geometrically perfect.
  - Why cubes evolved is still unknown, and the script says so.
  - The study was done on bare-nosed (common) wombats.

## Day 4 - History: Oxford is older than the Aztec Empire
- Verified claim: teaching existed at Oxford by 1096. The Mexica founded Tenochtitlan in 1325, and Spanish forces captured it in 1521.
- Source: Smithsonian, https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/oxford-university-is-older-than-the-aztecs-1529607/
- Nuance:
  - Oxford became a formal university gradually (it was chartered and organized in the 1200s).
  - The Aztec Empire proper (the Triple Alliance) dates from 1428, even later. The script uses the 1325 city founding, which is the conservative date.
  - The Mexica people existed before 1325.
  - The script's "more than two hundred years" covers the gap from 1096 to 1325, which is 229 years.

## Day 5 - Physics: your head is older than your feet
- Verified claim: in 2010, NIST compared two aluminum-ion optical clocks. The clock raised about 33 cm (about one foot) ran slightly faster, as general relativity predicts. Over a 79-year life, being one foot higher adds about 90 billionths of a second.
- Source: https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2010/09/nist-pair-aluminum-atomic-clocks-reveal-einsteins-relativity-personal
- Nuance:
  - The 90 ns figure is for a one-foot height difference, and the script frames it that way. A real head-to-feet gap (~1.7 m) would give a somewhat larger but still tiny difference.
  - Later clocks (JILA 2022) measured the effect across about 1 mm. That is not used in the script.

## Day 6 - Ocean: lakes at the bottom of the ocean
- Verified claim: brine pools are "essentially underwater lakes" (NOAA). They form because brine is saltier and denser than seawater, so it collects on the seafloor. They are found in the Gulf of Mexico, the Red Sea and the Mediterranean (Eos). The Orca Basin brine is about 8 times saltier than normal seawater (Eos). A 2006 NOAA dive found a brine lake at about 2,330 m with a shoreline, beach ridges and a swash zone. The submersible's movement made waves that broke on that shoreline "much like storm waves on a sandy coast".
- Sources:
  - https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/multimedia/daily-image/media/20200720.html
  - https://origin.oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/explorations/06mexico/logs/may31/may31.html
  - https://eos.org/articles/waves-of-deadly-brine-can-slosh-after-submarine-landslides
- Nuance:
  - The "8x saltier" figure applies to Orca Basin specifically. Salinity varies between pools.
  - The waves in the 2006 dive were small and caused by the submersible. They were not natural surf.
  - Brine pools are toxic to most animals that wander in. The script leaves this out to stay non-grim.
- Replaced candidate: "dark oxygen" (Nature Geoscience 2024). It was dropped because the claim is unreplicated and actively disputed, including a published critique to be presented at Seabed Minerals 2026 (https://geoforskning.no/?p=60315). It is not safe as a "verified true" fact yet.

## Day 7 - Psychology / odd science: you can't tickle yourself
- Verified claim: the cerebellum predicts the sensory result of your own movements and dampens activity in the somatosensory and anterior cingulate cortex, so self-tickling feels weaker. In robot experiments (Blakemore et al., UCL), people moved a robot that touched their own palm. Adding a delay between the movement and the touch made it feel more ticklish, and longer delays made it feel more ticklish still.
- Sources:
  - https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/why-cant-a-person-tickle/
  - https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/headlines/2016/sep/why-cant-i-tickle-myself
- Nuance:
  - Self-tickling is weakened, not always zero.
  - The original robot studies used delays of a few hundred milliseconds at most and also varied the direction of the stroke. The script says only "a tiny delay". The exact ms values were not verified in the fetched sources.
